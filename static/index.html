THE PROBLEM · THE GAP SLIDE 02 / 05

Maps show where.
Forecasts show when.
GeoGuide brings both together.

Most weather forecasts do not combine travel time, wind, air quality (PM2.5/AQI), and terrain to help people choose a suitable outdoor place and time. A forecast is only one part of making a good outdoor decision.

REAL USER QUERY (SLIDE 02)

“Where can I go for a run near Koregaon Park tomorrow, and what time would be best?”

GeoGuide recommends a suitable place and time, explains what to prepare for, and flags conditions that may make going outdoors a poor choice.

1. Standard Maps

Shows "Where" Only

  • Finds nearby parks, trails, and walking distances.
  • Ignores whether the park is choked with traffic PM2.5 at 8 PM.
  • No awareness of heat index, UV spikes, or user asthma sensitivity.
Missing: Temporal & Environmental Context
2. Weather Forecasts

Shows "When" Only

  • Gives city-wide hourly temperature and rain probability.
  • Does not compare tree-canopy parks vs. exposed highway paths.
  • Doesn't factor in OSRM travel effort or terrain elevation slope.
Missing: Spatial & Micro-Terrain Context
Llama-3.2-3B
3. GeoGuide Synthesis

Unifies Place + Time

  • Multi-Park × Multi-Window Matrix: Scores candidate parks across time slots.
  • Health-Aware: Adjusts weights for asthma, running pace, or heat tolerance.
  • Grounded Evidence: Cites live Open-Meteo, Overpass & OSRM numbers.
Result: Actionable Place–Time Decision

STAGE 01 · NATURAL LANGUAGE QUESTION

Capturing Activity, Location & Health Context

Users ask naturally without filling complex GIS forms. Whether they mention mild asthma, a 5km morning run, or avoiding steep inclines, GeoGuide captures both explicit and implicit constraints.
